From discovery to decision

How to use a LitBuy spreadsheet.

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used when researching purchases through a shopping agent. This page provides a HipobuyFind selection for comparison. An agent name in a catalog title does not confirm that a product is supported by that service.

01 / BUILD YOUR SHORTLIST

Save the exact reference.

Choose a product type and budget, then record its item ID, selected color and size. Keep the original marketplace URL when available. Similar photos can appear across different listings, so compare the actual option rather than the headline alone.

02 / VERIFY THE PURCHASE

Check the listing and service.

Open HipobuyFind to locate your item. Retrieve the source listing and confirm that your chosen agent supports it. Check the current option price, seller’s size chart, product restrictions and return conditions before paying.

03 / REVIEW BEFORE SHIPPING

Inspect your own order.

Compare warehouse photos with the order details. Ask for useful measurements and close-ups when needed. Resolve a wrong size, missing piece or visible defect before authorizing the international parcel.

Clear answers

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ.

Is this the official LitBuy spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent discovery guide. The catalog selection comes from HipobuyFind, and the shopping buttons open HipobuyFind. This page has no access to LitBuy accounts, orders or customer support records.

Do these prices include international shipping?

No. The displayed CNY amounts are catalog reference prices. Confirm the selected option, domestic delivery, international shipping, taxes and any service fees separately. A low starting price may correspond to a different option or quantity.

Are the product images QC photos?

No. These are source catalog listing images. Request and review photos of your own order before approving shipment. A photo alone does not establish authenticity, material quality or long-term durability.

Can every product be purchased through LitBuy?

Compatibility has not been verified. Retrieve the original marketplace link and confirm support and product restrictions with your chosen purchasing service before paying. The links on this page lead to HipobuyFind rather than a LitBuy checkout.

How do I find the exact product after clicking?

Record the source item ID displayed on the card, then locate that reference in the destination catalog. Product titles may change, so keep both the ID and the selected option in your notes.

How current is this catalog selection?

The eight references are a snapshot reviewed on 19 November 2026. This page does not synchronize live stock or prices. Open the destination catalog and original listing to verify current information.

What should I do if a listing disappears?

Search using the source item ID and product description. If you find a similar item, compare the seller, measurements, options and price again. A matching image does not prove it is the same listing.
